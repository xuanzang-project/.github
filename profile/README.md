# Xuanzang 玄奘

**Ancient Journeys. Modern Systems.**

Xuanzang is an open-source technology community dedicated to building, exploring, and maintaining open technology across software, electronic design automation (EDA), embedded systems, and computer hardware.

Inspired by the pursuit of knowledge, Xuanzang aims to create an environment where developers, engineers, researchers, and enthusiasts can collaborate to build useful technology and share knowledge.

## Our Vision

To build an open, collaborative technology ecosystem that grows beyond its founding contributors and enables people to learn, research, develop, and maintain technology together.

## Technical Directions

- **Software:** Systems software, developer tools, libraries, toolchains, and infrastructure.
- **EDA:** Open-source tools and infrastructure for electronic design automation.
- **Embedded Systems:** Firmware, drivers, microcontrollers, RTOS, and hardware/software integration.
- **Hardware & VLSI:** CPU architecture, FPGA development, RTL, SoCs, ASIC design, and physical design.

## Where We Start

Our first major technical direction is **open-source EDA**. We aim to develop useful tools and contribute to the existing open-source EDA ecosystem, building on and working alongside projects such as Yosys, OpenROAD, and OpenSTA.

EDA is our starting point, not the limit of Xuanzang.

## Our Principles

- **Open by Default** — Open source, documentation, and knowledge wherever practical.
- **Community-Driven** — Projects should grow beyond their original contributors.
- **Build and Learn** — Beginners and experienced engineers can work together.
- **Technical Independence** — Build solutions when existing technology does not meet our needs.
- **Distributed Ownership** — Encourage shared maintenance, transparent decisions, and long-term sustainability.

## Contributing

You do not need to be an expert to contribute. Contributions can include:

- Software development and engineering
- Algorithms and technical research
- Hardware and EDA development
- Testing and benchmarking
- Documentation and tutorials
- Development infrastructure and community support

A contribution does not have to be code.

## Our Long-Term Direction

We intend to grow progressively from software and EDA into broader developer tooling, embedded systems, FPGA development, CPU architecture, SoCs, and VLSI.

These are long-term directions, not promises to build everything simultaneously.

**Start with something useful. Build the community around it. Then see how far we can go.**
